Tile board

Drag a shape from the tray onto the grid, or click a preset to fill the whole board with a repeating pattern. Click a placed tile to rotate it 90 degrees. Double click a tile to clear it. Color A and Color B set the colors for the next piece you drag in from the tray only.

Presets

Tray

Shapes: arc-tl, arc-tr, arc-bl, arc-br are quarter circles anchored at that corner. diag-tlbr and diag-trbl are corner to corner triangle splits. solid is a flat fill. Presets tile by row and column remainder, so they repeat cleanly at any board size.
